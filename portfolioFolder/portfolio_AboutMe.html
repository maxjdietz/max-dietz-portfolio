Welcome to Suburbia!

Welcome to Suburbia! is a 2 player, co-op, online multiplayer psychological horror game about 2 young siblings that are stuck in an infinetly repeating suburban neighborhood. This game features many complex systems, including multiplayer syncronization, procedrally generated content, dynamic sound systems, and a surreal horror experience!
